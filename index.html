ABOUT / WORKING METHOD

Curiosity is useful.
Evidence is better.

I’m a software engineering graduate moving toward security operations, with a particular interest in how raw telemetry becomes a useful detection—and how an analyst can verify what an alert actually means.

My projects move between SIEM engineering, endpoint visibility, packet analysis, threat intelligence, digital forensics, and response automation. I like building the lab, generating controlled test activity, following the evidence across tools, and documenting what worked, what failed, and why.

My current direction is SOC analysis and incident triage, with a longer-term interest in detection engineering. I aim to keep claims tied to observable results rather than treating a configured tool as proof that a control works.
