06 About

  

    
I came into security from software engineering, and I still approach it like an engineer: build the thing, break it, then measure whether the detection actually works. I learn best on real traffic and real logs, which is why most of my projects run on live labs rather than slides.

    
Right now I am going deeper on log analysis, detection rules and incident investigation using Wazuh, Sysmon, Suricata, Zeek and Wireshark, and exploring where automation (n8n and AI classification) helps a SOC and where a human must still decide.

    
My goal is to grow as a SOC analyst, starting with alert triage and incident response, then move into detection engineering.
